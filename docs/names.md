# Product name candidates: English, Spanish, and two-word compounds

**100 distinct candidates in this round. Availability has not been checked.** Candidates may already name other products; these are brainstorming options, not cleared brands.

The user's benchmark is **Devbox**: short, descriptive, easy to type, and broad enough for a complete working environment. Devbox itself is already an established development tool, so use it as a style reference rather than a recommendation. The product organizes workspaces, services, tasks, private state, and URLs; names should not reduce it to a router.

This supersedes the previous Japanese/multilingual direction. The [earlier 120 candidates](names-0.1.md) are preserved as a historical brainstorm, not the current shortlist.

## Curated shortlist

| Rank | Candidate | Why it fits | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Devdeck** | A control/work surface for multiple development workspaces and services; compact like Devbox | Needs conflict checks; the deck metaphor is less literal than box |
| 2 | **Devyard** | One place holding several independent development environments | Yard metaphor may feel less technical |
| 3 | **Stackyard** | Multiple complete service stacks, each with its own working area | Stack terminology may suggest orchestration more than source organization |
| 4 | **Benchbox** | A contained developer workbench; close to the Devbox naming shape | Does not explicitly say dev; no security-sandbox claim implied |
| 5 | **Workdock** | Workspaces and services brought together at a dock | Could suggest a launcher/window dock |
| 6 | **Stackdeck** | A control surface for stacks, logs, and lifecycle | Similar sound to other stack products |
| 7 | **Taller** | Spanish workshop; a natural broad name for a complete development workspace | Common word; English pronunciation/spelling ambiguity |
| 8 | **Parcela** | Spanish plot/parcel: each workspace has its own area | Not immediately recognizable as developer tooling |
| 9 | **Tallerbox** | Workshop + box, explicitly blending the requested directions | Longer and intentionally a hybrid, not an established Spanish word |
| 10 | **Colmena** | Spanish hive: many organized working areas/services | Can suggest agent orchestration, which is not the core product |

Current preference: **Devdeck** or **Devyard** for English; **Taller** for Spanish. The rest is a broad pool to identify the user's preferred word family, not a claim that all 100 are equally good.

CLI spellings are lowercase, regardless of brand capitalization. Compare `devdeck up`, `devyard workspace list`, `stackyard run -- bin/dev`, `benchbox status`, and `taller logs`. These are illustrative names/commands, not installed programs.

## Spanish associations

Brief naming associations, not exhaustive definitions:

- **Taller:** workshop.
- **Parcela:** parcel/plot of land.
- **Estancia:** stay/dwelling; also an estate in some regions.
- **Recinto:** enclosure or defined area.
- **Anden:** ASCII spelling of andén, a station platform.
- **Cuadra:** block or stable, depending on region/context.
- **Solar:** plot of land; the English solar-energy association is a naming tradeoff.
- **Huerto:** kitchen/vegetable garden.
- **Refugio:** shelter/refuge.
- **Colmena:** hive.

## Selection gate

Choose three to five favorites or a word family first. Then verify executable/package/repository conflicts, relevant trademark/brand issues, pronunciation/connotations, and desired domain availability. Do not rename the codebase, package, or state directory before choosing and checking a name. Product branding never changes the app-specific URL `shirabe.localhost`.
